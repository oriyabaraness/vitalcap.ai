Why now

Resilience moved from best practice to board-level obligation

Regulation

EU and UK rules now require financial firms to map critical services, dependencies and impact tolerances — and to prove it.

  • EU DORA — in force 16 Jan 2023; fully applicable from 17 Jan 2025
  • UK FCA PS21/3 — rules effective 31 Mar 2022; full mapping, testing and impact-tolerance compliance by 31 Mar 2025
  • UK PRA SS1/21 — aligned operational-resilience expectations for PRA-authorised firms (same 31 Mar 2025 implementation deadline)

Concentration

Operations depend on a handful of cloud and SaaS vendors. One vendor outage now ripples across thousands of firms at once.

AI is ready

Language models can now read organizational data and open-source signals and draft a plan in minutes instead of months.

How it works

From org chart to recovery targets, automatically

1 · Positions 2 · AI 3 · Minimum service staff 4 · Critical application + RTO / RPO / MAD

RTO = how fast an app must recover · RPO = how much data you can lose · MAD = how long the business process can be down. Rule: an app’s RTO must fit inside the MAD of every process that relies on it. Figures are illustrative.
