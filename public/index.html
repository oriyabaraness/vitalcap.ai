      From org chart to recovery targets, automatically

      1 · Positions 2 · AI 3 · Minimum service staff 4 · Critical application + RTO / RPO / MAD

      RTO = how fast an app must recover · RPO = how much data you can lose · MAD = how long the business process can be down. Rule: an app’s RTO must fit inside the MAD of every process that relies on it. Figures are illustrative.
